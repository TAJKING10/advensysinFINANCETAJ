### **Step 2: Google Search Console (MOST IMPORTANT) - Do This TODAY**

**This is how you get on Google Search - Your #1 priority!**

1. **Go to:** https://search.google.com/search-console

2. **Add Your Property:**
   - Click "Add Property"
   - Select "URL prefix"
   - Enter: `https://advensys-in-finance.com`

3. **Verify Ownership:**
   - Choose "HTML file" method
   - Download the verification file
   - Upload to your OVHcloud `www` folder (same level as index.html)
   - Click "Verify"

4. **Submit Your Sitemap:**
   - Click "Sitemaps" in left menu
   - Enter: `sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
   - **This speeds up indexing from 4 weeks to 3-7 days!**

5. **Request Indexing for Important Pages:**
   - In top bar, enter URLs one by one:
     - `https://advensys-in-finance.com/`
     - `https://advensys-in-finance.com/services`
     - `https://advensys-in-finance.com/services/broker-in-bank`
   - Click "Request Indexing" for each
   - **This can get you indexed in 24-48 hours!**
